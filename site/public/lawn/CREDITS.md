# LAWN WARS credits

Every model in `public/lawn/models/` (the five heroes, Biscuit the dog, the garden robots, the seagulls, all props,
vehicles and furniture, and the house kit: window, door and garage walls, gable roof, balcony, stairs and portico) and
every tiling texture in `public/lawn/textures/` is an original asset made for this project. The houses, the street,
particles and sound are generated in code.

The only third-party content left is the hero animation set, retargeted onto the heroes' skeletons:
