Changes made: joints renamed and the library movement clips retargeted onto the player skeleton; the players'
jersey and trouser textures replaced by colour-free shading maps (kit-top.webp, kit-bottom.webp, derived from the
originals with the real-world text, number and logos painted out) that the game tints with its fictional teams'
colours; the helmet's logo plates removed; the stumps straightened into one stump and one bail; the stadium's
playing field removed so the game's own ground shows through.

Ground textures (grass.webp, pitch.webp, concrete.webp) are ambientCG Grass005, Ground109 and Concrete034 (CC0).
