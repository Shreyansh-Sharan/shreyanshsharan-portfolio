# Cricket: 3D model credits

The players (two fielders, the batter, the wicket-keeper and the umpire), the bat, ball, stumps and bails, and the
venue pieces (grandstand section, pavilion, sight screen, scoreboard, boundary board, floodlight tower, pitch roller)
are original models made for this project. They were converted to meshopt-compressed glTF with WebP textures,
rescaled, re-rigged to the game's skeleton and given kit masks so the game can paint its fictional teams' colours,
names and numbers at runtime. The advertising names on the boundary boards are invented.

The players' movement clips (idle, walk, jog, sprint, throw, pick-up, dive, celebrations) come from Quaternius'
Universal Animation Library (CC0) and were retargeted onto each body's skeleton; the cricket strokes, bowling
actions, keeping and catches are procedural.

Ground textures (grass.webp, pitch.webp, concrete.webp) are ambientCG Grass005, Ground109 and Concrete034 (CC0).
