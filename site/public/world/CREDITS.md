# 3D models used on the island

Every building, landmark, tree, vehicle and piece of interior furniture on the island is an original model made for this project: the harbour lane, the castle arcade and its hall, Neon City's towers, brownstones and cyberpunk stacks, the walk-in stadium (grandstands, corner stands, the players' tunnel, floodlights and the bowl seen from afar), Khotyn Keep's walls, towers, gatehouse, keep and great hall (throne, banquet tables, chandeliers, hearth, armour, weapon racks, stained glass and tapestries), the temple and its stone lanterns, the village below the keep, the cabins, the round-door house, the lighthouse, the shipwreck, the airship, the hover car, the metro train, the bridge, the drone, the neon signs and the trees. They were optimised with gltf-transform (simplification, WebP textures, meshopt compression) to fit a web page. No downloaded (Creative Commons) models remain, so there are no third-party credits to list here; the in-world credits panel says the same.

The low-poly city props (taxi, box truck, streetlights, signs, shelters, shops, bollards, dumpsters, crates and barrels) are from the site owner's licensed 3D asset library. The car is the portfolio's own racer model; the first island's landmarks (distillery, silos, crystals, clock tower, observatory and so on) and the character rigs are the site owner's.
