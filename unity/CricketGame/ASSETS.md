rotations plus the pelvis translation and drop the finger channels.

## Generated in code (no files)

- **Stadium** (`View/Stadium.cs`, `View/MeshKit.cs`): oval with mowing stripes, pitch, creases and footmarks, rope and
  boards, sight screens, stands, roofs, floodlight towers and sky. It is built at runtime and time-sliced, so the menu shows first.
- **Crowd** (`View/Crowd.cs`): GPU-instanced spectators that stand and cheer.
- **Bat, pads, gloves, helmet, ball, stumps and bails** (`View/Cricketer.cs`, `View/MatchView.cs`).
- **Cricket-specific movement** (`View/Cricketer.cs`, `View/Ik.cs`): a procedural animation layer over the base clips
  for batting strokes, bowling actions, keeping, catching, diving and umpire signals.
- **All audio** (`Fx/Sfx.cs`): bat on ball, edges, pad, stumps and bails, the bounce, catches, crowd ambience, roars,
  applause and the crowd's "ooh". It is synthesised at startup, so there are no audio files in the build.
- **Wagon wheel, pitch map and Hawk-Eye graphics** (`UI/Hud.Cards.cs`, `View/MatchView.cs`).

## Teams and players

All eight teams, every player name and every kit are fictional, and the name pools are invented (`Sim/Teams.cs`). No
real players, leagues, boards or logos are used.
